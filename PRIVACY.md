# Privacy

Oh My Proxy is fully local proxy control. It has no account, analytics, advertising, or developer backend.

Your proxy settings, routing rules, and check results stay in browser extension storage. Proxy passwords are not encrypted by the extension.

When enabled, the browser sends matching traffic through your selected proxy. Checking a proxy loads https://example.com/ through that proxy to measure whether it responds and how fast. The extension contacts no other server, and it does not use third-party IP lookup services.

The developer does not receive, sell, or share your extension data.
